What Are Dental Crowns?

A Dental Crown, often called a Tooth Crown or Tooth Cap, is a custom-made restoration that completely covers a damaged tooth. Crowns strengthen weakened teeth, restore their shape, and improve their appearance while protecting them from further damage.Dental crowns are commonly recommended for: Cracked teeth Severely worn teeth Large cavities Root canal-treated teeth Broken or chipped teeth Cosmetic smile improvements

Types of Dental Crowns Available

  • Porcelain Crowns:

    • Porcelain Crowns are known for their natural appearance and excellent aesthetics. They closely resemble natural tooth enamel, making them a popular choice for front teeth.

  • Ceramic Crowns:

    • Ceramic Crowns provide durability and a highly realistic appearance. They are ideal for patients seeking metal-free restorations.

  • Zirconia Crowns:

    • Zirconia Crowns are among the strongest crown materials available. They offer exceptional durability and are suitable for both front and back teeth.

  • Crown Restoration for Cracked Teeth:

    • A Cracked Tooth can cause discomfort, sensitivity, and increased risk of infection. Crown Restoration helps strengthen the damaged tooth, preventing further deterioration while restoring normal chewing function.
